## Full description

Keep your team connected to customers with KshanaAPI for mobile.

- Manage your shared inbox and customer conversations.
- Access campaigns, message templates, and automation workflows.
- Stay informed with workspace alerts and optional push notifications.
- Work with contacts, leads, businesses, and follow-ups.
- Review business reports and mobile usage analytics.
- Manage your profile and notification preferences.

Sign in with your KshanaAPI workspace account. Features depend on your role,
workspace configuration, and subscription. Push notifications require permission.
An internet connection is required.

## Review notes draft

KshanaAPI is a business workspace app. Reviewers need a dedicated production
review account with sample data and permissions for inbox, campaigns, templates,
automation, alerts, and reports. Supply its email, workspace slug, and password
privately in the store's review-access form. Do not commit credentials here.

Main tabs are Home, Inbox, Campaigns, Alerts, and Profile. The hamburger menu
provides templates, automations, contacts, follow-ups, and Analytics. Analytics
includes business reports and aggregate opt-in mobile usage. Profile contains
push permission controls, optional analytics consent, analytics deletion, policy
links, and an account-deletion request action. Embedded modules use the same
authenticated workspace as the native screens.

Do not send real customer messages or launch real campaigns during review.
Provide a demo workspace with safe recipients and sample data. Complete account
deletion handling and state its actual processing timeframe in reviewer notes.
